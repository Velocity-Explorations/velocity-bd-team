# Velocity Explorations — Boilerplate Sections

## Security Compliance

Velocity Explorations maintains active CMMC 2.0 Level 1 compliance and is registered in SPRS with a current NIST 800-171 self-assessment. Our personnel hold active security clearances up to TS/SCI, and we are pursuing a Top Secret Facility Clearance (FCL). For classified work, Velocity leverages existing SCIFs and government-provided systems. CUI is handled exclusively on government-provided platforms (e.g., Platform One Mattermost), ensuring no CUI is processed, stored, or transmitted on contractor systems.

## Quality Management

Velocity employs an Agile development methodology with iterative sprint cycles, continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD), and regular stakeholder reviews. Our quality process includes automated testing, peer code review, security scanning (SAST/DAST), and user acceptance testing with operational stakeholders. All deliverables undergo internal quality review before customer submission.

## Transition Approach

Velocity's transition methodology ensures minimal disruption to ongoing operations:
1. **Knowledge Transfer**: Comprehensive documentation, runbooks, and training materials developed throughout the period of performance
2. **Phased Handoff**: Gradual capability transfer with parallel operations period
3. **Training & Sustainment**: End-user training, administrator training, and maintenance documentation
4. **Post-Transition Support**: Available for consultation during the transition-out period to ensure continuity

## Small Business Participation

As a certified Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB), Velocity Explorations is committed to maximizing small business participation. When teaming, we maintain small business subcontracting plans that comply with FAR 19.7 requirements and actively seek SDB, SDVOSB, HUBZone, and 8(a) subcontractors for specialized capabilities.

## Data Rights

Unless otherwise specified in the contract, Velocity retains unlimited rights to all software and technical data developed under SBIR/STTR contracts in accordance with DFARS 252.227-7018. For non-SBIR work, data rights are negotiated per the applicable DFARS/FAR clauses and clearly documented in the contract data requirements list (CDRL).
