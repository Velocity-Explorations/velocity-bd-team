The Velocity Explorations presentation theme. Rules and examples are in README.md; how to use these files in other tools is in HOW-TO-USE.md. Sizes are for a 1920×1080 slide; halve them at 960 wide.

Colors

ground

#1e1e1e

Slide and page background

surface

#262626

Cards, row cards, stat tiles, roadmap bars and upcoming step badges, always with a line border

surface-recessed

#1a1a1a

Cards in the column that matters less (out of scope, assumptions to validate)

line

#333333

Card borders, hairline rules between list rows, the footer rule, chart axes and the | separator.

line-strong

#434343

Tag borders and the border of later roadmap phases.

ink

#f5f5f5

Titles, names and primary text on ground, surface and surface-recessed (15:1 on ground)

ink-secondary

#bebebe

Supporting copy, statements, subtitles and tag text on ground, surface and surface-recessed (9:1 on ground, 8:1 on surface).

ink-muted

#757575

Section labels, dates, meta, footers and chart values

ink-faint

#555555

Excluded or deprioritized chart items and axis ticks only

accent

#ffcb03

Velocity yellow

on-accent

#1e1e1e

Text on an accent fill: phase block labels, the current step numeral, chart marker numerals (11:1).

glow-cool

rgba(0, 39, 76, 0.25)

Cool radial glow over ground, transparent at 60%

glow-warm

rgba(255, 203, 3, 0.06)

Warm radial glow (the accent at 6%) over ground, transparent at 60%

Components

CoverSlide

The opening slide: logo and classification marking along the top, the briefing's name in the middle, the company line at the foot.

Guidelines · Open example

ContentSlide

The working slide: eyebrow, title and accent rule at the top, content from y=310, an optional footnote at the foot.

Guidelines · Open example

SectionSlide

A divider between parts of a briefing: eyebrow, one display title, a short rule and one line, centered on the warm glow.

Guidelines · Open example

ClosingSlide

The last slide: logo, display title, rule, the subject line and the classification marking, centered, with the company line at the foot.

Guidelines · Open example

SlideHeader

The top of every content slide: eyebrow, title, optional subtitle, and the accent rule.

Guidelines · Open example

Card

A bordered `surface` panel for one person, statement or list: the building block of most content slides.

Guidelines · Open example

StatTile

A tile for one figure: a label, then the value, with an optional unit line.

Guidelines · Open example

ClassificationCallout

A full-width card that states the classification of the work in `accent`, with one line of handling guidance.

Guidelines · Open example

BulletList

Lines marked with square bullets: `accent` squares for our items or the main list, grey squares for anyone else's.

Guidelines · Open example

NumberedList

Two columns of zero-padded numbers and lines, a hairline under each, for an agenda or any ordered set of up to eight.

Guidelines · Open example

MilestoneList

Milestone codes and names in rows, separated by hairlines.

Guidelines · Open example

StepList

Numbered next steps: a square badge, a title and one line of detail, with hairlines between. The current step's badge is filled with `accent`.

Guidelines · Open example

Roadmap

A delivery plan as stacked rows: a phase block naming the phase and its months, then a bar naming the work and its parts.

Guidelines · Open example

Tag

A small bordered label for a feature, tool or channel.

Guidelines · Open example

PriorityMatrix

Value against complexity: four named quadrants, numbered markers, and a legend naming each marker with its scores.

Guidelines · Open example

Footnote

One line at the foot of a slide: a lead-in label and a sentence, or meta items, split by a `|` separator.

Guidelines · Open example
