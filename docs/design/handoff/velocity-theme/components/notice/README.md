# Notice

Part of the Velocity Theme app components (26 Oct 2026). Tokens are in tokens-app.css; the card is in card.css.

- A full-width bar at the top of the page: the tone’s wash, a 1px tone line, radius 4px.
- A 76px column with a 6px square and the word (Done, Refused, Note) in the tone, then one sentence saying what happened and what to do next, with at most one underlined link.
- role status for done, role alert for refused. A 28px close button on the right.
